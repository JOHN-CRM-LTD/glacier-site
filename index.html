The rink

A winter world,
indoors.

Glacier is a full-size indoor arena built for one thing: great skating. Spotless ice, proper skate-fit staff and room to find your glide — whether it’s your first lap or your thousandth.

  • NHL-standard 60 × 30 m ice pad
  • 500 pairs of skates, child 8 to adult 14
  • Warm cafe & rink-side viewing
  • Free parking & step-free access
See it for yourself

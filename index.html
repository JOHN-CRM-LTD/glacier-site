The rink

A winter world,
indoors.

Glacier is a full-size indoor arena built for one thing: great skating. Spotless ice, proper skate-fit staff and room to find your glide — whether it’s your first lap or your thousandth.

  • NHL-standard 60 × 30 m ice pad
  • 500 pairs of skates, child 8 to adult 14
  • Warm cafe & rink-side viewing
  • Free parking & step-free access
See it for yourself

What’s on

Pick your session

Six ways onto the ice, every week of the year. Walk in or book ahead — your call.

Public skate

Open ice for all ages and abilities. Skate hire included, helpers on hand for wobbly first-timers.

Daily · 10:00 – 20:00

Learn to skate

Structured six-week courses with qualified coaches, from first steps to confident gliding.

From age 4 · 6-week blocks

Figure skating

Quiet freestyle ice for spins, jumps and choreography. Club members and drop-ins welcome.

Tue & Thu · 18:00 – 19:30

Ice hockey

Skills drills and scrimmage nights for juniors and adults. Full kit hire available at reception.

Wed & Sun · skills + game

Parties & events

Birthday packages with private rink time, party room and a host to run the games.

Private hire · from 10 guests

Disco nights

Lights down, music up. The full rink under disco lights with a resident DJ on the deck.

Fri & Sat · 19:00 – 22:00
